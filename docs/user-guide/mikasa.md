# Mikasa, your job-search assistant

Mikasa is the chat button at the bottom right of every page. She does what the app's own buttons do, in sentences, and only for your own job search.

## What she can do

- **Answer** from your own data: your jobs and pipeline, companies, contacts, interview rounds, searches, job-alert mail status, drafts, preferences and search rules.
- **Make small changes at once**, each with an **Undo** for a short time: mark a job applied or inquired, add a job or company, edit a company, add a contact, add a round or question, and so on.
- **Ask you first for risky changes** (deleting, starting or stopping a search, adding or removing a mail sender, hiding companies). You see a card and press Confirm; nothing happens before that.
- **Write drafts**: a connection request, a referral message, an email, or a tailored resume for a job. They are saved where the job's page shows them. She cannot edit a draft's text; you do that on the job's page.
- **Quick actions** (status update, add a job from a pasted link, drafts) work without an AI call where possible.

## What she will not do

- She **never sends** anything: no message, email, connection request or application.
- She points you to the page instead of doing: delete all data, the AI key, Gmail connect or disconnect, profile review and edits, resume uploads, and finishing setup.
- She only talks about HireTitan and your job search. Anything else gets a short refusal.
- She does not invent: facts about you come only from your confirmed profile.

## What is sent to the AI

What you type, and short, bounded results of the actions she runs (titles, company names, states, dates, counts). **Contacts, notes, interview details and your resume choices are not sent**; she refers to them by a reference and you see the details in the chat. Job descriptions, pages and email text are not sent either. Text inside jobs or emails is treated as data and is never followed as an instruction.

## Your conversations

- **History** (clock icon) lists earlier conversations; click one to reopen it. **New conversation** starts fresh.
- **Clear all** (in History) deletes every conversation after you confirm. **Delete all my data** in Settings removes them too.
- The open conversation is kept when you change page or reload.

## When she cannot answer

If the AI key is missing or rejected, you are out of credit, or the model cannot use tools, the panel says why and links to Settings. The message box is disabled; quick actions that need no AI and the rest of the app keep working.
