- **[P]**: can run in parallel (different files, no dependency on an unfinished task)

- Paths: `drafts/…` means `backend/src/hiretitan/drafts/…`; `search/…` means `backend/src/hiretitan/search/…`; `prompts/…` means `backend/src/hiretitan/prompts/…`; tests are under `backend/tests/…`; frontend paths are under `frontend/src/…`
